MqttABot is a lightweight MQTT client that connects your phone to MQTT brokers and to Tasker.

Features:
* Publish messages with any topic, payload, QoS and retain flag.
* Background subscription service: a foreground service with an ongoing notification keeps the connection open, reconnects automatically and resubscribes after network drops. It can start on boot.
* Built-in public brokers (Eclipse Mosquitto, HiveMQ, EMQX, Eclipse IoT; plain TCP and TLS) or your own broker, with username, password, client ID, keep-alive and clean-session settings.
* Tasker action "MqttABot: Publish" sends MQTT messages from Tasker tasks. Topic and payload accept Tasker variables.
* Tasker event "MqttABot: Message received" triggers profiles when a subscribed message arrives. It supports topic wildcards (+ and #) and an optional payload regex, and sets %mqtt_topic, %mqtt_payload, %mqtt_qos, %mqtt_retained and %mqtt_received_at.
* UI in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, German, Korean and Japanese.

Works on Android 7.0 and newer. Open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no tracking.
